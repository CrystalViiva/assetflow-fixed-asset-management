# AssetFlow commercial working drafts

These drafts describe current features and are not final pricing, customer commitments, legal terms, or approved service guarantees.

## Product one-page overview

**AssetFlow — Fixed Asset Management Software**

AssetFlow helps finance and operations teams manage asset acquisition, capitalization, straight-line depreciation, custody, transfers, maintenance, physical verification, disposal, reports, and audit history through one controlled workflow.

**For:** finance teams, asset accountants, operations and facilities managers, construction and engineering businesses, and multi-location organizations managing equipment, vehicles, IT assets, and other fixed assets.

**What teams can do:**

- Maintain a searchable, organization-scoped asset register.
- Record componentized acquisition and controlled capitalization.
- Generate and post straight-line depreciation through accounting periods.
- Track custody separately from asset placement and transfers.
- Record maintenance work without silently changing accounting values.
- Capture physical verification observations and review deterministic control findings.
- Preserve report snapshots, private exports, and application audit events.

**Accounting note:** These are IAS 16-aligned workflows, not a claim of full IAS 16/IFRS compliance. Current scope does not include component depreciation, impairment ledger, revaluation, estimate revisions, decommissioning accounting, or multi-currency accounting.

**Next step:** Request a guided demonstration and discuss a managed onboarding pilot. Self-service trials and local sandbox billing can be demonstrated where configured. No live collection is enabled.

## Demo script (20 minutes)

1. Ask how the prospect currently identifies assets, tracks location/custody, handles depreciation, and reconciles physical counts.
2. Show the asset register and organization scope; explain that the public preview is illustrative.
3. Create an asset and record acquisition components; show backend-provided exact money values.
4. Show capitalization and a straight-line depreciation schedule/posting; call out the documented accounting boundaries.
5. Show custody assignment versus completed placement transfer.
6. Show maintenance work, then explain it is operational and does not automatically change capitalized cost.
7. Show verification observations and assurance findings; demonstrate that they do not silently mutate asset/accounting data.
8. Generate a report snapshot and private export; explain snapshot as-of semantics.
9. Close with onboarding requirements, data migration scope, support model, security limitations, and a pilot proposal.

Use synthetic demo data. Do not present mock content as a customer tenant or describe sandbox transactions as live payments or claim hosted controls without deployment evidence.

## Discovery questionnaire

- How many assets, locations, entities, and active users need to be in scope?
- What identifiers and source registers are used today? What data quality issues exist?
- Which currencies, accounting periods, depreciation methods, useful lives, residual value policies, and approval controls apply?
- Which accounting system must receive exports or reconciliations?
- How are custody, transfers, maintenance, physical verification, and disposal approved today?
- What evidence, audit, retention, privacy, and residency requirements apply?
- Which roles can create assets, approve capitalization, post depreciation, approve disposals, and export records?
- What migration, training, support hours, availability, recovery, and incident response commitments are required?
- Who will approve commercial terms, data processing terms, and accounting configuration?

## Pilot proposal outline

1. Customer goals and agreed success measures.
2. Pilot tenant, users, locations, asset volume, and dates.
3. Included lifecycle workflows and explicit exclusions.
4. Data mapping, migration responsibility, validation, and customer sign-off.
5. Training, named contacts, support route, and response expectations.
6. Security/privacy assumptions and data processing terms for legal review.
7. Fees and payment terms as separately approved business decisions.
8. Exit plan, supported exports, retention and deletion timeline.
9. Acceptance criteria: reconciled opening asset values, role tests, sample transactions, reports/exports, and customer approval.

## Implementation and onboarding checklist

- Confirm legal entity, organization identifier, currency, and timezone.
- Name a customer administrator, finance owner, operations owner, and support contacts.
- Agree data import template, opening balance date, asset tags, cost/residual/life policies, departments, and locations.
- Validate a sample import; reconcile cost, accumulated depreciation, and book value to the source ledger.
- Configure role/approval policy and demonstrate cross-tenant denial.
- Train administrators, asset managers, and accountants with synthetic examples.
- Test customer exports and support process.
- Obtain written acceptance of migrated scope, limitations, and unresolved exceptions.

## Support policy and service objectives (draft)

Support channel, hours, severity definitions, response targets, maintenance windows, availability objective, backup RPO/RTO, security notification timing, and escalation contacts must be set before contract publication. Until measured in a selected hosted environment, do not promise uptime percentages, response times, data loss limits, or recovery durations. The customer should report the workspace, time, workflow, request ID, and impact without sending passwords, tokens, payment details, or unnecessary personal data.

## Pricing strategy (decision framework)

Treat pricing as configurable commercial policy, not code constants. Validate willingness to pay with pilots and account for implementation/support, hosting, storage, email, backups, taxes, payment costs, and customer asset/user ranges. Candidate dimensions for research include organization tier, active users, asset volume, storage, and support/onboarding. Avoid caps that block access to records or create destructive expiry behavior. Publish exact plans only after approving NGN/international pricing, billing interval, taxes, refund/cancellation, trial, grace period, and support inclusion.

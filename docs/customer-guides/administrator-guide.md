# Organization administrator guide (draft)

Organization administrators manage users and organization references within their own tenant. They do not create or inspect other organizations, set platform-wide plans, or act as AssetFlow platform operators.

## Before inviting a team

Agree naming conventions for asset tags, departments, locations, and asset categories. Decide which roles can create assets, post depreciation, approve transfers/disposals, and view reports under your internal control policy. Use separate named accounts and review access when staff change roles.

## User access

Use the organization user directory to review roles, active status, and department assignment. Keep at least one active organization administrator. Disable access promptly when a user's employment or responsibility ends. Password-based invitation/activation and account recovery are not part of the audited baseline; until those flows are deployed, follow the onboarding process supplied by the service operator and never transmit passwords in ordinary email or chat.

## Operational controls

Review pending accounting periods and approvals, reconcile asset records with physical verification results, and export information only for authorized business purposes. Contact the service operator for organization suspension, data export assistance, or offboarding. Do not attempt to change records directly in the database.

## Limitations

An organization administrator cannot access another tenant, grant platform operator privileges, or directly change subscription entitlements. Billing and self-service plan management are not available in the current product baseline.

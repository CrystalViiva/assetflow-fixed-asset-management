# AssetFlow FAQ

### Is AssetFlow fully IFRS compliant?

No. AssetFlow supports IAS 16-aligned fixed-asset workflows but is not a complete IFRS accounting system or legal/accounting compliance certification. Current boundaries include straight-line depreciation only, with no component depreciation, revaluation, impairment ledger, estimate revisions, decommissioning accounting, or multi-currency accounting.

### Does physical verification change the asset record?

No. Verification captures observed facts and exceptions. Authorized users review findings and make any required master-data change through the appropriate controlled workflow.

### Does a transfer close custody?

No. A completed placement transfer and a custody assignment are separate records. Custody is changed through assignment/return workflows.

### Can I see another organization's assets?

The authenticated API scopes customer data to the user's organization. Contact support if a record appears in the wrong workspace; do not share screenshots containing private customer information through an unapproved channel.

### Can I reset my password or create a workspace online?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. Company registration is available where self-service has been enabled; otherwise request managed onboarding. Verification is required before a new company account is activated.

### Are subscription payments supported?

Local sandbox checkout and subscription states are functional and visibly labeled. They do not collect money. A Paystack test transaction adapter is implemented but has not been verified against a merchant account. Live payment collection is disabled. Managed hosting fees require separately agreed terms.

### How do I export data?

Use the Reports area to create a supported report snapshot and request an authorized CSV or JSON export. Download links require your authenticated workspace session. Ask your administrator or service contact for help with an offboarding export.

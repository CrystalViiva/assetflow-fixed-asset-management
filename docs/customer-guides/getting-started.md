# AssetFlow getting started

AssetFlow helps a team keep fixed asset records, accounting workflows, custody, maintenance, verification, and disposal connected. Your administrator will provide your workspace access after your organization is onboarded.

## First workspace visit

1. Open the workspace link provided by your organization and sign in with your individual account.
2. Confirm that the organization shown is yours. Contact your administrator if it is wrong.
3. Review departments and locations before registering assets so records use consistent values.
4. Register an asset with its identifier, category, location, owner/custodian information, and supporting acquisition details available to you.
5. Record acquisition components and ask an authorized accountant/administrator to review capitalization details.
6. Use assignment for custody, transfer for a completed change in placement, and maintenance for operational work. These records serve different purposes.
7. Use verification to record what was physically observed. Resolve exceptions through the appropriate review workflow; a verification result does not silently change accounting values.
8. Use reports and exports for the records you are authorized to access. Exports are private workspace data.

## Accounting scope

The current accounting implementation provides componentized acquisition costs, capitalization, straight-line depreciation, accounting periods and controlled posting, carrying value, and disposal gain/loss. It does not provide all IFRS/IAS 16 requirements, impairment accounting, revaluation, component depreciation, or multiple accounting currencies. Confirm accounting judgments with your organization's qualified finance professionals.

## Access and support

Do not share passwords or use another person's account. Ask your administrator to invite or deactivate users and assign roles/departments. Contact your designated AssetFlow support contact with the workspace, approximate time, affected workflow, and any request ID; do not email passwords, reset links, tokens, or asset documents unless an approved secure support channel is provided.

Managed customers receive an administrator activation invitation. Where enabled, Get started also supports self-service company registration and email verification. Billing controls are explicitly sandbox-only. See [common workflows](workflows.md) for invitation, recovery, setup and export instructions.

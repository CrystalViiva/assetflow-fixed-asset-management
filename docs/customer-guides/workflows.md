# Common customer workflows

## Invitations and password recovery

An administrator opens **Users & Roles**, enters an invitation email, selects a role and optionally a department, then sends the invitation. Pending invitations can be revoked. The recipient follows the email link within 48 hours, chooses a password and signs in. An existing account address is not reassigned to a different company. Contact your administrator when a link expires or has been replaced.

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to request recovery. The response protects account privacy and does not confirm whether an address exists. Use the newest email within one hour. Resetting or changing your password ends prior sessions. Under **Settings**, view your account and company details, change your password, or open the Getting started checklist.

## Asset manager

1. Configure categories, departments and locations with your administrator.
2. Register the asset with a stable asset tag and verified acquisition details.
3. Record custody through assignment/return. Use transfers to change placement through its approval workflow; a placement change does not close custody.
4. Plan maintenance, open work orders and record operational costs. These costs do not automatically change the capitalized asset basis.
5. Run physical verification, record observations and attach authorized private evidence. Verification and assurance findings do not silently change the register or accounting records.
6. Submit disposal requests for the separate approval process. Retain supporting evidence under your company's policy.

## Accountant

Review acquisition components and directly attributable cost judgments before capitalization. Check useful life, residual value and supported straight-line method against company policy. Review schedules and accounting periods, then post through the authorized workflow. Repeated posting requests do not create duplicate ledger entries. Review disposal proceeds, carrying value and gain/loss at derecognition.

Use live reports for current records. Create a report snapshot when a durable as-of view is needed, then request a CSV or JSON export. The export is private and requires an authorized session. Historical snapshots do not change when the live asset is subsequently disposed. Reconcile outputs with your finance ledger and retain your accounting approvals.

AssetFlow supports IAS 16-aligned workflows with documented conventions. It does not implement full IFRS accounting, revaluation, impairment, independent component depreciation, estimate revisions or currency conversion. Your qualified finance team remains responsible for accounting judgments.

## Trial and billing

Where self-service is enabled, **Get started** leads to company registration. Verify your email, choose an available trial plan and password, then sign in. Settings links to setup tasks, invitations, the asset register and billing. Refreshing a pending signup does not activate an unverified company.

Billing shows the server's plan, status, usage, billing contact and transaction history. **Sandbox** controls simulate outcomes without charging money. Sample prices are test values. No live payment collection is enabled in this release. Managed customers use agreed commercial terms with the operator. Trial or paid-period expiry restricts writes while preserving supported reads and exports; contact your administrator for help.

## Support and export

Give your designated support contact the company name, affected workflow, approximate time, request ID when shown and business impact. Never send passwords, activation links or payment card details. The operator sees account and operational metadata; exceptional access to customer records requires a separate authorized process. Request offboarding assistance before workspace suspension so supported reports can be exported and evidence retention agreed.
